# Consent and recording form

Print one per participant. The signed copy stays with the moderator, in the organisation's research folder, and is never scanned into this repository. This repository only ever holds the participant code.

---

## Tiles usability study: taking part

**Participant code:** P\_\_\_ **Date:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ **Round:** baseline (U1.01) / second (U6.07)

**What this is.** We are testing Tiles, a program for plant data, by watching people use it. We are testing the software, not you. The session takes about 60 minutes: some tasks in Tiles, a short questionnaire and a conversation.

**What we record.** If you agree below: the laptop's screen, and your voice as you think aloud. Not your face. The note-taker writes down what happens, under your participant code.

**What happens to it.**

- The recording and notes are kept by the study team, in the organisation's research folder, and deleted 12 months after the round's report is written.
- The report and anything in Tiles' repository name you only by your code (P01, P02…) and your role. Your name and this form stay with the moderator.
- Anything you type in Tiles during the session is made-up data on a study site and is deleted after the session. Please don't type real passwords or personal details.
- Taking part has nothing to do with your performance at work, and what you say isn't passed to your manager.

**You can stop.** You can pause or stop at any time, without giving a reason. If you stop, we delete your recording and notes if you ask, until the report is written.

## For the moderator

- Copy the ticks into `participants.csv` (`consent_screen`, `consent_voice`, `consent_quotes`, `consent_clips`: `yes` or `no`). Start the recording only once the form is signed, and record only what is ticked.
- Without the voice tick, take written notes only; without the screen tick, don't record at all.
- Quote and show clips only as ticked. A participant who asks to be removed: delete their recording and notes, and their rows in the round's CSV files.
