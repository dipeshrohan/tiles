# Lessons learned: holding furnace heater element on DC-02, 22 April 2026

**What happened:** DC-02 stopped at 09:42 on Wednesday 22 April 2026 (DT-FUR: holding furnace fault).
The MES logged: "Furnace temperature low, heater element replaced". The cell was down 9.6 hours, about 29,853 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 16 April. `hf02.heater_power` averaged 46.3 a week before and 64.8 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a heater element of the holding furnace aged and failed; the bath cooled.

**Actions:** measure the element resistances when the heater power runs above 58 kW (SOP-HF-03).
