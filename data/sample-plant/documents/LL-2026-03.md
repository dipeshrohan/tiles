# Lessons learned: holding furnace heater element on DC-01, 06 March 2026

**What happened:** DC-01 stopped at 12:32 on Friday 06 March 2026 (DT-FUR: holding furnace fault).
The MES logged: "Furnace temperature low, heater element replaced". The cell was down 6.4 hours, about 19,747 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 28 February. `hf01.heater_power` averaged 36.3 a week before and 65.3 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a heater element of the holding furnace aged and failed; the bath cooled.

**Actions:** measure the element resistances when the heater power runs above 58 kW (SOP-HF-03).
