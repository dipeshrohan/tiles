# Lessons learned: holding furnace heater element on DC-04, 13 January 2026

**What happened:** DC-04 stopped at 18:32 on Tuesday 13 January 2026 (DT-FUR: holding furnace fault).
The MES logged: "Furnace temperature low, heater element replaced". The cell was down 7.4 hours, about 17,768 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 19 December. `hf04.heater_power` averaged 49.5 a week before and 70.9 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a heater element of the holding furnace aged and failed; the bath cooled.

**Actions:** measure the element resistances when the heater power runs above 58 kW (SOP-HF-03).
