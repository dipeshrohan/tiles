# Lessons learned: plunger tip and shot sleeve on DC-01, 23 September 2026

**What happened:** DC-01 stopped at 21:28 on Wednesday 23 September 2026 (DT-PLG: plunger seized or tip damaged).
The MES logged: "Plunger stuck in the sleeve, tip changed". The cell was down 11.1 hours, about 34,534 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 20 September. `dc01.plunger_pressure_1st` averaged 16.5 a week before and 25.2 in the last day.

**Root cause:** the plunger tip scored in the shot sleeve and friction rose shot by shot until it seized.

**Actions:** watch the slow-phase pressure and change the tip above 30 bar (SOP-DC-04).
