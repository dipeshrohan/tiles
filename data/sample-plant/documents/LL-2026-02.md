# Lessons learned: holding furnace heater element on DC-03, 01 March 2026

**What happened:** DC-03 stopped at 23:27 on Sunday 01 March 2026 (DT-FUR: holding furnace fault).
The MES logged: "Holding furnace heater failed: element changed". The cell was down 6.8 hours, about 18,413 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 18 February. `hf03.heater_power` averaged 40.8 a week before and 58.5 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a heater element of the holding furnace aged and failed; the bath cooled.

**Actions:** measure the element resistances when the heater power runs above 58 kW (SOP-HF-03).
