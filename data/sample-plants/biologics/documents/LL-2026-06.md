# Lessons learned: spindle bearings on CF-01, 27 June 2026

**What happened:** CF-01 stopped at 10:34 on Saturday 27 June 2026 (DT-CEN: centrifuge fault: spindle bearing).
The MES logged: "Centrifuge vibration high: bearing set changed". It was down 16.9 hours, about 20,228 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 03 June. `cf01.vibration` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** the centrifuge's spindle bearings wore; its vibration rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** plan the bearing change at 4.5 mm/s (SOP-DSP-01).
