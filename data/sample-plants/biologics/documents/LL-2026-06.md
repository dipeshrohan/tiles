# Lessons learned: dosing pump on FL-01, 25 July 2026

**What happened:** FL-01 stopped at 21:59 on Saturday 25 July 2026 (DT-FIL: filler fault: dosing pump).
The MES logged: "Checkweigher rejects: pump piston worn, changed". It was down 6.6 hours, about 11,809 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 16 July. `fl01.fill_weight` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** a dosing pump's piston wore; its needle under-filled the vials.

**Actions:** watch the fill weights by needle, not only the checkweigher's rejects (SOP-FF-01).
