# Lessons learned: agitator mechanical seal on BR-02, 13 June 2026

**What happened:** BR-02 stopped at 02:29 on Saturday 13 June 2026 (DT-SEAL: agitator mechanical seal failed).
The MES logged: "Seal barrier fluid alarm: mechanical seal replaced". It was down 29.7 hours, about 924,154 EUR. Batch B26066 was lost with it.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 22 May. `br02.seal_loss` averaged 3.58 a week before and 9.60 at its last reading before the stop.

**Root cause:** the agitator's mechanical seal faces wore; the barrier fluid loss rose until the seal leaked.

**Actions:** replace the seal at the turnaround once its barrier fluid loss passes 3 mL/h (SOP-USP-03).
