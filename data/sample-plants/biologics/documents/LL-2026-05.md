# Lessons learned: spindle bearings on CF-01, 23 June 2026

**What happened:** CF-01 stopped at 00:00 on Tuesday 23 June 2026 (DT-CEN: centrifuge fault: spindle bearing).
The MES logged: "Vibration trip at start-up: spindle bearings replaced". It was down 16.5 hours, about 19,778 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 03 June. `cf01.vibration` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** the centrifuge's spindle bearings wore; its vibration rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** trend the vibration from harvest to harvest: the change planned at 4.5 mm/s (SOP-DSP-01) comes too late for bearings that wear fast.
