# Lessons learned: agitator mechanical seal on BR-04, 28 September 2026

**What happened:** BR-04 stopped at 20:00 on Monday 28 September 2026 (DT-SEAL: agitator mechanical seal failed).
The MES logged: "Seal leaking into the vessel: agitator seal changed". It was down 29.7 hours, about 924,195 EUR. Batch B26077 was lost with it.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 17 September. `br04.seal_loss` averaged 0.97 a week before and 8.99 at its last reading before the stop.

**Root cause:** the agitator's mechanical seal faces wore; the barrier fluid loss rose until the seal leaked.

**Actions:** replace the seal at the turnaround once its barrier fluid loss passes 3 mL/h (SOP-USP-03).
