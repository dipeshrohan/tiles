# Lessons learned: wfi still on WFI-01, 22 May 2026

**What happened:** WFI-01 stopped at 01:05 on Friday 22 May 2026 (DT-WFI: wfi out of specification).
The MES logged: "WFI out of specification: still's heat exchanger cleaned, loop sanitised". It was down 13.9 hours, about 20,815 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 14 May. `wfi01.conductivity` averaged 0.42 a week before and 1.08 at its last reading before the stop.

**Root cause:** the still's heat exchanger fouled; the distillate's conductivity and TOC crept up.

**Actions:** clean the still's heat exchanger when the conductivity passes 1.0 µS/cm (SOP-UT-01).
