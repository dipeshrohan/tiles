# Lessons learned: spindle bearings on CF-01, 22 November 2025

**What happened:** CF-01 stopped at 02:11 on Saturday 22 November 2025 (DT-CEN: centrifuge fault: spindle bearing).
The MES logged: "Vibration trip at start-up: spindle bearings replaced". It was down 20.6 hours, about 24,736 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 27 October. `cf01.vibration` averaged 4.38 a week before and 2.46 at its last reading before the stop.

**Root cause:** the centrifuge's spindle bearings wore; its vibration rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** plan the bearing change at 4.5 mm/s (SOP-DSP-01).
