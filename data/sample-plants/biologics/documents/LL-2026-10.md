# Lessons learned: door seal and isolation valve on LY-01, 29 September 2026

**What happened:** LY-01 stopped at 01:06 on Tuesday 29 September 2026 (DT-LYO: lyophiliser leak test failed: vacuum repaired).
The MES logged: "Leak rate over 20 µbar·L/s: isolation valve rebuilt". It was down 24.5 hours, about 78,533 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 08 September. `ly01.leak_rate` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** the lyophiliser's door seal wore; the chamber leaked and ran at a higher pressure.

**Actions:** stop at a leak rate of 20 µbar·L/s, and trend it from cycle to cycle (SOP-FF-02).
