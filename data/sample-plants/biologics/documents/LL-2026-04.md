# Lessons learned: agitator mechanical seal on BR-02, 13 June 2026

**What happened:** BR-02 stopped at 02:54 on Saturday 13 June 2026 (DT-SEAL: agitator mechanical seal failed).
The MES logged: "Seal barrier fluid alarm: mechanical seal replaced". It was down 29.7 hours, about 924,154 EUR. Batch B26062 was lost with it.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 22 May. `br02.seal_loss` averaged 3.58 a week before and 9.60 at its last reading before the stop.

**Root cause:** the agitator's mechanical seal faces wore; the barrier fluid loss rose until the seal leaked.

**Actions:** trend the barrier fluid loss through each batch: a seal past 3 mL/h at the turnaround is changed then (SOP-USP-03), but one that wears fast fails before it.
