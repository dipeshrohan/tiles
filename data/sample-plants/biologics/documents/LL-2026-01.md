# Lessons learned: cex column on CS-02, 06 January 2026

**What happened:** CS-02 stopped at 15:50 on Tuesday 06 January 2026 (DT-COL: chromatography column repacked: bed compressed).
The MES logged: "Pre-use test failed: CEX column repacked". It was down 20.4 hours, about 24,459 EUR.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 22 December. `cs02.cex_asymmetry` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** the CEX column's bed compressed; its peaks tailed and it cleared fewer aggregates.

**Actions:** repack at an asymmetry of 1.6, and trend the drug substance's aggregates (SOP-DSP-04).
