# Lessons learned: slot die on CT-A1, 17 June 2026

**What happened:** CT-A1 stopped at 07:02 on Wednesday 17 June 2026 (DT-DIE: slot die: lip build-up, die cleaned).
The MES logged: "Coating weight out of tolerance across the web: die cleaned". It was down 3.7 hours, about 6,608 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 11 June. `cta1.coating_weight_cv` averaged 0.9 a week before and 2.9 the day before.

**Root cause:** dried slurry built up on the slot die lips, making streaks and an uneven coating across the web.

**Actions:** clean the die when the coating weight's variation passes 2.0 % (SOP-EL-01).
