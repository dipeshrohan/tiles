# Lessons learned: work roll bearing on CL-A1, 15 August 2026

**What happened:** CL-A1 stopped at 00:29 on Saturday 15 August 2026 (DT-CAL: calender fault: roll bearing).
The MES logged: "Thickness out of tolerance: bearing changed". It was down 5.6 hours, about 5,000 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 06 July. `cla1.bearing_vibration` averaged 3.1 a week before and 5.3 the day before.

**Root cause:** a work roll bearing wore; the roll vibrated and the thickness varied.

**Actions:** plan the bearing change at 4.5 mm/s (SOP-EL-03).
