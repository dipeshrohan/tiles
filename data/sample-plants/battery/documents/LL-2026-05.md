# Lessons learned: dryer zone 3 fan on CT-A1, 03 March 2026

**What happened:** CT-A1 stopped at 01:39 on Tuesday 03 March 2026 (DT-DRY: dryer fault: fan or heater).
The MES logged: "Dryer zone 3 too cold: fan motor changed". It was down 4.4 hours, about 7,941 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 12 February. `cta1.dryer_fan_current` averaged 16.1 a week before and 20.1 the day before.

**Root cause:** the zone 3 fan's bearings wore; the zone ran colder and the electrode left the dryer wet.

**Actions:** change the fan bearings when the fan current passes 17 A (SOP-EL-02).
