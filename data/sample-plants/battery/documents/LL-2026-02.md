# Lessons learned: channel contacts on FM-01, 26 January 2026

**What happened:** FM-01 stopped at 13:00 on Monday 26 January 2026 (DT-FRM: formation rack fault: channel contacts).
The MES logged: "Formation faults: channel contacts replaced". It was down 8.3 hours, about 1,248 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 14 December. `fm01.contact_resistance` averaged 5.7 a week before and 7.7 the day before.

**Root cause:** the channels' contact probes wore; contact resistance rose and cells ran hot.

**Actions:** service the probes above 6 mΩ (SOP-FA-01).
