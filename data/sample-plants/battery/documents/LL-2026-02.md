# Lessons learned: ultrasonic horn on UW-01, 25 January 2026

**What happened:** UW-01 stopped at 01:09 on Sunday 25 January 2026 (DT-WLD: tab welder fault: horn).
The MES logged: "Low weld energy margin: horn and anvil changed". It was down 3.4 hours, about 4,814 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 19 January. `uw01.weld_energy` averaged 655.6 a week before and 841.2 the day before.

**Root cause:** the ultrasonic horn cracked; the welds needed more energy and grew weaker.

**Actions:** inspect the horn above 850 J (SOP-AS-02).
