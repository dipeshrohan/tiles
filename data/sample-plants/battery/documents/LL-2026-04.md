# Lessons learned: channel contacts on FM-02, 05 February 2026

**What happened:** FM-02 stopped at 23:38 on Thursday 05 February 2026 (DT-FRM: formation rack fault: channel contacts).
The MES logged: "Formation faults: channel contacts replaced". It was down 8.6 hours, about 1,296 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 23 January. `fm02.contact_resistance` averaged 3.0 a week before and 6.7 the day before.

**Root cause:** the channels' contact probes wore; contact resistance rose and cells ran hot.

**Actions:** service the probes above 6 mΩ (SOP-FA-01).
