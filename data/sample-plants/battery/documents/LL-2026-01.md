# Lessons learned: desiccant wheel on DR-01, 13 January 2026

**What happened:** DR-01 stopped at 08:30 on Tuesday 13 January 2026 (DT-DRM: dry room out of specification).
The MES logged: "Dew point above -30 °C: assembly stopped, wheel regenerated". It was down 3.7 hours, about 10,237 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 30 December. `dh01.wheel_dp` averaged 237.3 a week before and 363.5 the day before.

**Root cause:** the dehumidifier's desiccant wheel loaded up; the dry room's dew point rose.

**Actions:** regenerate the wheel at 380 Pa, before the dew point climbs (SOP-DR-01).
