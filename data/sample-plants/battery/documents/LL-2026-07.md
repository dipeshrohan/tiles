# Lessons learned: ultrasonic horn on UW-01, 17 June 2026

**What happened:** UW-01 stopped at 12:37 on Wednesday 17 June 2026 (DT-WLD: tab welder fault: horn).
The MES logged: "Weld rejects: horn cracked, changed". It was down 3.3 hours, about 4,602 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 15 June. `uw01.weld_energy` averaged 619.9 a week before and 673.8 the day before.

**Root cause:** the ultrasonic horn cracked; the welds needed more energy and grew weaker.

**Actions:** inspect the horn above 850 J (SOP-AS-02).
