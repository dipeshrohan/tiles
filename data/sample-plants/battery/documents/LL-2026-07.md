# Lessons learned: channel contacts on FM-01, 11 June 2026

**What happened:** FM-01 stopped at 14:35 on Thursday 11 June 2026 (DT-FRM: formation rack fault: channel contacts).
The MES logged: "Rack contact resistance high: probes changed". It was down 6.5 hours, about 971 EUR of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 18 May. `fm01.contact_resistance` averaged 4.6 a week before and 7.5 the day before.

**Root cause:** the channels' contact probes wore; contact resistance rose and cells ran hot.

**Actions:** service the probes above 6 mΩ (SOP-FA-01).
