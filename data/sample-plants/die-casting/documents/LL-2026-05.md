# Lessons learned: plunger tip and shot sleeve on DC-02, 15 April 2026

**What happened:** DC-02 stopped at 06:11 on Wednesday 15 April 2026 (DT-PLG: plunger seized or tip damaged).
The MES logged: "Plunger stuck in the sleeve, tip changed". The cell was down 5.5 hours, about 17,205 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 11 April. `dc02.plunger_pressure_1st` averaged 15.6 a week before and 22.9 in the last day.

**Root cause:** the plunger tip scored in the shot sleeve and friction rose shot by shot until it seized.

**Actions:** watch the slow-phase pressure and change the tip above 30 bar (SOP-DC-04).
