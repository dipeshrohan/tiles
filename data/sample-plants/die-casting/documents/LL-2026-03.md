# Lessons learned: die cooling, moving half on DC-03, 14 March 2026

**What happened:** DC-03 stopped at 11:51 on Saturday 14 March 2026 (DT-DIE: die cleaning: soldering or blocked cooling).
The MES logged: "Die too hot, cooling channel blocked: cleaned". The cell was down 5.9 hours, about 16,030 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 03 March. `dc03.cooling_flow_moving` averaged 24.3 a week before and 16.5 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a cooling channel in the moving die half scaled up; the die ran hot and the parts soldered.

**Actions:** watch the moving-half flow; descale below 32 l/min (SOP-DC-09).
