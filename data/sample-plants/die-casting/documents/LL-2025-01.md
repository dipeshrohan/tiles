# Lessons learned: die cooling, moving half on DC-01, 28 October 2025

**What happened:** DC-01 stopped at 19:30 on Tuesday 28 October 2025 (DT-DIE: die cleaning: soldering or blocked cooling).
The MES logged: "Die too hot, cooling channel blocked: cleaned". The cell was down 5.9 hours, about 18,430 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 14 October. `dc01.cooling_flow_moving` averaged 33.7 a week before and 21.2 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a cooling channel in the moving die half scaled up; the die ran hot and the parts soldered.

**Actions:** watch the moving-half flow; descale below 32 l/min (SOP-DC-09).
