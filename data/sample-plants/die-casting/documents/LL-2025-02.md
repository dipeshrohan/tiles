# Lessons learned: plunger tip and shot sleeve on DC-01, 14 November 2025

**What happened:** DC-01 stopped at 08:29 on Friday 14 November 2025 (DT-PLG: plunger seized or tip damaged).
The MES logged: "Plunger seized: tip and sleeve changed". The cell was down 4.8 hours, about 14,849 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 07 November. `dc01.plunger_pressure_1st` averaged 18.0 a week before and 30.5 in the last day.

**Root cause:** the plunger tip scored in the shot sleeve and friction rose shot by shot until it seized.

**Actions:** watch the slow-phase pressure and change the tip above 30 bar (SOP-DC-04).
