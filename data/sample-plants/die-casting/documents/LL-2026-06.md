# Lessons learned: holding furnace heater element on DC-02, 24 April 2026

**What happened:** DC-02 stopped at 00:18 on Friday 24 April 2026 (DT-FUR: holding furnace fault).
The MES logged: "Holding furnace heater failed: element changed". The cell was down 8.7 hours, about 26,936 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 16 April. `hf02.heater_power` averaged 39.5 a week before and 62.9 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a heater element of the holding furnace aged and failed; the bath cooled.

**Actions:** measure the element resistances when the heater power runs above 58 kW (SOP-HF-03).
