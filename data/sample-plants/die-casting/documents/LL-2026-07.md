# Lessons learned: plunger tip and shot sleeve on DC-04, 24 April 2026

**What happened:** DC-04 stopped at 21:55 on Friday 24 April 2026 (DT-PLG: plunger seized or tip damaged).
The MES logged: "Tip scored, sleeve honed and tip changed". The cell was down 9.4 hours, about 22,631 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 21 April. `dc04.plunger_pressure_1st` averaged 15.9 a week before and 27.7 in the last day.

**Root cause:** the plunger tip scored in the shot sleeve and friction rose shot by shot until it seized.

**Actions:** watch the slow-phase pressure and change the tip above 30 bar (SOP-DC-04).
