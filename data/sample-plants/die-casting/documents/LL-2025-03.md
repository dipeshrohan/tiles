# Lessons learned: die cooling, moving half on DC-01, 08 December 2025

**What happened:** DC-01 stopped at 01:00 on Monday 08 December 2025 (DT-DIE: die cleaning: soldering or blocked cooling).
The MES logged: "Soldering on the moving half: die cleaned, channel descaled". The cell was down 8.8 hours, about 27,382 EUR
of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 27 November. `dc01.cooling_flow_moving` averaged 26.4 a week before and 16.8 in the last day.

**Root cause:** a cooling channel in the moving die half scaled up; the die ran hot and the parts soldered.

**Actions:** watch the moving-half flow; descale below 32 l/min (SOP-DC-09).
