# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-03, 23 March 2026

**What happened:** CVD-03 stopped at 09:00 on Monday 23 March 2026 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Base pressure too high to start: tube flange seals replaced". It was down 5.5 hours, about 8,306 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 04 March. `cvd03.oxygen` averaged 9.5 a week before and 24.9 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
