# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-04, 21 September 2026

**What happened:** CVD-04 stopped at 09:00 on Monday 21 September 2026 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Base pressure too high to start: tube flange seals replaced". It was down 8.7 hours, about 13,102 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 25 August. `cvd04.oxygen` averaged 12.7 a week before and 24.9 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
