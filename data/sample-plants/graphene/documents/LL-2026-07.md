# Lessons learned: methane mass flow controller on CVD-02, 14 August 2026

**What happened:** CVD-02 stopped at 06:57 on Friday 14 August 2026 (DT-MFC: gas flow fault: mass flow controller).
The MES logged: "Transmittance out of tolerance: methane MFC found reading low, recalibrated". It was down 6.6 hours, about 9,883 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 30 July. `in01.transmittance` was not logged then.

**Root cause:** the methane controller drifted: it read 30 sccm while letting more through, growing bilayer patches.

**Actions:** recalibrate when transmittance stays below 97.2 % on two runs (SOP-CVD-03).
