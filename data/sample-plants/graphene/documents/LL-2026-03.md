# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-01, 27 April 2026

**What happened:** CVD-01 stopped at 09:00 on Monday 27 April 2026 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Leak at the tube flange: O-rings changed". It was down 8.8 hours, about 13,179 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 23 March. `cvd01.oxygen` averaged 14.5 a week before and 24.9 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
