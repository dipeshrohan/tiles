# Lessons learned: zone 2 heating element on CVD-01, 27 March 2026

**What happened:** CVD-01 stopped at 11:20 on Friday 27 March 2026 (DT-HTR: furnace heater fault).
The MES logged: "Zone 2 could not hold 1,035 °C: heating element replaced". It was down 9.6 hours, about 14,384 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 18 February. `cvd01.heater_power` averaged 18.9 a week before and 26.6 the day before.

**Root cause:** zone 2's heating element aged; it drew more power and at last could not hold 1,035 °C.

**Actions:** plan the element's replacement when its power passes 23 kW (SOP-CVD-01).
