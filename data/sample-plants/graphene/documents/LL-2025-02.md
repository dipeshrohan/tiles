# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-03, 03 November 2025

**What happened:** CVD-03 stopped at 13:00 on Monday 03 November 2025 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Base pressure too high to start: tube flange seals replaced". It was down 5.6 hours, about 8,335 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 16 October. `cvd03.oxygen` averaged 7.2 a week before and 24.8 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
