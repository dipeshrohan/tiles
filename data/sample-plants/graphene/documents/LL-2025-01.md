# Lessons learned: scrubber packing on SC-01, 29 October 2025

**What happened:** SC-01 stopped at 18:20 on Wednesday 29 October 2025 (DT-EXH: exhaust scrubber out: cvd hall stopped).
The MES logged: "Scrubber pressure drop trip: furnaces put in safe state, packing cleaned". It was down 7.7 hours, about 46,315 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 08 October. `sc01.differential_pressure` averaged 8.6 a week before and 13.4 the day before.

**Root cause:** the scrubber's packing fouled with the exhaust's deposits; its pressure drop rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** clean the packing at 12 mbar, before it trips the hall (SOP-EHS-01).
