# Lessons learned: zone 2 heating element on CVD-02, 09 June 2026

**What happened:** CVD-02 stopped at 07:00 on Tuesday 09 June 2026 (DT-HTR: furnace heater fault).
The MES logged: "Zone 2 could not hold 1,035 °C: heating element replaced". It was down 10.8 hours, about 16,226 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 10 April. `cvd02.heater_power` averaged 20.4 a week before and 11.3 the day before.

**Root cause:** zone 2's heating element aged; it drew more power and at last could not hold 1,035 °C.

**Actions:** plan the element's replacement when its power passes 23 kW (SOP-CVD-01).
