# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-02, 29 July 2026

**What happened:** CVD-02 stopped at 04:20 on Wednesday 29 July 2026 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Base pressure too high to start: tube flange seals replaced". It was down 5.5 hours, about 8,286 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 10 July. `cvd02.oxygen` averaged 6.3 a week before and 22.3 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
