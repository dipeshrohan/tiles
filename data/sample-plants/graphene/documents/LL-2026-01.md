# Lessons learned: zone 2 heating element on CVD-04, 04 March 2026

**What happened:** CVD-04 stopped at 06:34 on Wednesday 04 March 2026 (DT-HTR: furnace heater fault).
The MES logged: "Heater element open circuit: replaced". It was down 8.8 hours, about 13,182 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 27 January. `cvd04.heater_power` averaged 22.0 a week before and 26.8 the day before.

**Root cause:** zone 2's heating element aged; it drew more power and at last could not hold 1,035 °C.

**Actions:** plan the element's replacement when its power passes 23 kW (SOP-CVD-01).
