# Lessons learned: quartz tube seals on CVD-04, 16 December 2025

**What happened:** CVD-04 stopped at 21:00 on Tuesday 16 December 2025 (DT-VAC: vacuum leak: tube seals).
The MES logged: "Base pressure too high to start: tube flange seals replaced". It was down 7.0 hours, about 10,468 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 01 December. `cvd04.oxygen` averaged 5.4 a week before and 19.1 the day before.

**Root cause:** the tube's flange seals hardened; air leaked in and the oxygen etched the graphene as it grew.

**Actions:** leak-test when the exhaust's oxygen passes 4 ppm (SOP-CVD-02).
