# Lessons learned: dry vacuum pump on CVD-02, 07 April 2026

**What happened:** CVD-02 stopped at 15:00 on Tuesday 07 April 2026 (DT-PMP: vacuum pump fault).
The MES logged: "Pump seized during pump-down: exchanged for the spare". It was down 5.7 hours, about 8,616 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 12 March. `cvd02.pump_current` averaged 11.8 a week before and 13.3 the day before.

**Root cause:** the dry pump's bearings wore; its current rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** plan the pump's exchange at 11.5 A (SOP-CVD-02).
