# Lessons learned: dry vacuum pump on CVD-02, 07 July 2026

**What happened:** CVD-02 stopped at 15:00 on Tuesday 07 July 2026 (DT-PMP: vacuum pump fault).
The MES logged: "Vacuum pump tripped on overcurrent: exchanged". It was down 7.3 hours, about 10,894 GBP of lost contribution.

**What the data showed:** the problem started around 29 June. `cvd02.pump_current` averaged 9.0 a week before and 12.0 the day before.

**Root cause:** the dry pump's bearings wore; its current rose until it tripped.

**Actions:** plan the pump's exchange at 11.5 A (SOP-CVD-02).
